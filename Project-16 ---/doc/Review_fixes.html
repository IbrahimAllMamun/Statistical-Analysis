100% anti-dsDNA contradicts a minimum SLEDAI-2K of 1

Partly fixed
Reviewer comment (verbatim)

2. "100% anti-dsDNA positive" contradicts a minimum SLEDAI-2K of 1. In SLEDAI-2K, raised DNA binding scores 2 points if the descriptor is present at the time of the visit or in the preceding 10 days. If every patient was anti-dsDNA positive at assessment, no patient could score below 2. So either the antibody results are historical, or the SLEDAI scoring left out the immunological items. Either way, the scoring is suspect. (clinicaltrials)

Explanation and fixes

What the reviewer is saying: An anti-dsDNA-positive patient scores at least 2 on SLEDAI-2K, so a 100% positive cohort cannot have a minimum score of 1. Either the antibody results are old, or the immunological items were left out of the score.
Verdict: Correct. The 100% figure is also wrong.

Fixed values

  • Anti-dsDNA positive: 100% → 92%.
  • Patients with SLEDAI-2K = 1 are all recorded as anti-dsDNA positive, and in 34% of patients the recorded SLEDAI-2K is lower than the points their own recorded descriptors imply.
  • The antibody column is undated; the ACR and SLICC columns are explicitly “at diagnosis”. The serology is most likely from diagnosis.

Still needed from the client

  • Date of the anti-dsDNA and complement tests relative to the study visit, and whether the SLEDAI-2K immunological items were scored. If they were not, re-score from case-record forms.

Needs data
Reviewer comment (verbatim)

5. The adherence finding is confounded by newly diagnosed patients, probably fatally. 48% were newly diagnosed. Their SLEDAI-2K reflects untreated disease at presentation, so adherence cannot have caused it. Unadjusted confounding, overlapping measures, diagnostics

Partly fixed
Reviewer comment (verbatim)

6. Unadjusted confounding and overlapping measures. The model leaves out age, sex, disease duration, new-diagnosis status, prednisolone dose (a known driver of both mood and fatigue), neuropsychiatric lupus and organ involvement. Meanwhile it includes haemoglobin and CRP, which have no clear rationale (CRP is notoriously unreliable in SLE). PHQ-9 contains fatigue, sleep and appetite items, so PHQ-9 and FSS partly measure the same thing. Required additions:

  • VIFs to check collinearity, plus R² and adjusted R²;
  • residual diagnostics;

What the reviewer is saying: The model leaves out obvious confounders (age, sex, duration, steroid dose), includes Hb and CRP without a stated reason, and uses PHQ-9 and FSS, which overlap. The reviewer wants VIFs, R², residual checks and methods suited to skewed data.

Effect sizes out of line with the literature

Partly fixed
Reviewer comment (verbatim)

7. The effect sizes are out of line with the literature. An r of 0.736 between PHQ-9 and SLEDAI-2K would be among the strongest ever reported. The authors' own reference 6 (Liao 2022) is the best comparison: it studied 325 patients with PHQ-9 and reported no correlation coefficient. Its SLEDAI-2K cut-off for predicting depression was 8.5, with an AUC of only 0.660, which is a modest association. The authors' reference 20 (Mertz) also contradicts them: the PGA (p=0.004) but not the SELENA-SLEDAI (p=0.43) was significantly associated with fatigue. A Saudi outpatient study (Lupus 2018;27:327–332) found that disease activity did not correlate with medication non-adherence. The Discussion has to address why these results are so much stronger. Without good reasons, extremely tidy, strong correlations should make the editor more suspicious, not less. (unica) (nih)

Explanation and fixes

What the reviewer is saying: Correlations of −0.67 and 0.74 are far stronger than published studies report, including the authors’ own references. The Discussion must explain why.
Verdict: A fair challenge. The fatigue result is verified; the other two cannot be checked yet.

  • FSS r = 0.429 is verified from the data. Spearman ρ = 0.412; in adults only (80% of patients), ρ = 0.404.
  • The fatigue association holds after adjustment (B = 5.40 per FSS point), and the corrected Discussion can compare it with Mertz 2022.
  • MMAS-8 (r = −0.669) and PHQ-9 (r = 0.736) cannot be checked until the item-level data are supplied.

Questionnaire language, validation, MMAS-8 licence

Client
Reviewer comment (verbatim)

9. Questionnaire language, validation and licensing.

  • PHQ-9: a validated Bangla version exists (Naher et al., DUJBS 2021, 321 Bangladeshi adults). The authors should use and cite it.
  • FSS and MMAS-8: the authors must say which Bangla versions were used and how they were validated.
  • MMAS-8 licence: two journals retracted papers this year for unauthorized use of the scale. In June 2025, a judge ruled that neither the MMAS-4 nor the MMAS-8 is entitled to copyright protection, but retractions continue. The 2008 MMAS-8 validation paper was itself retracted in 2023 after flaws with the reported sensitivity and specificity were identified. The authors should state whether they held a licence, and the journal should decide its position. (papers continue to face retractions for failure to license pricy tool +2)
Explanation and fixes

What the reviewer is saying: Each questionnaire’s Bangla version must be named and its validation cited, and the authors must say whether they held an MMAS-8 licence.
Verdict: Administrative. Only the authors can answer.

  • Which Bangla PHQ-9 was used. If it was Naher et al. (DUJBS 2021), cite it.
  • Source and validation of the Bangla FSS and MMAS-8, or the forward/back-translation procedure.
  • Whether an MMAS-8 licence was held (yes/no), stated truthfully; the journal decides policy.
